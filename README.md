# Integrated Health Management System

An AI-driven health management platform designed to track and improve your physical and mental well-being. Our system integrates essential health features in one platform, including food recognition, fitness tracking, sleep monitoring, and personalized recommendations.

## Features

- **AI-powered Food Recognition**: Using YOLO and CNN algorithms to identify meals and provide nutritional details (calories, macronutrients, portion sizes) through photos.
- **Seamless Wearable Device Integration**: Collect health metrics like heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen levels, and sleep patterns for holistic health insights.
- **Personalized Health Recommendations**: AI engine analyzes health data to offer tailored advice based on your unique health profile and goals.
- **Multi-user Roles**: Supports users, nutritionists, fitness trainers, healthcare providers, and administrators, each with specialized features.

## Benefits

- **Holistic Health Insights**: Track physical activities, nutrition, sleep, and mental wellness all in one platform.
- **Efficient Nutrition Tracking**: Eliminate manual data entry with automatic food recognition for better accuracy.
- **Community Support**: Connect with others on similar health journeys for motivation and support.

## Target Audience

- Health-conscious individuals managing overall wellness.
- People with specific health goals (e.g., weight management, fitness improvement).
- Nutrition professionals enhancing client services.
- Healthcare providers interested in remote patient monitoring.

## Technologies Used

- **YOLO (You Only Look Once)** and **CNN (Convolutional Neural Networks)** for food recognition.
- **AI and Machine Learning** for personalized recommendations.
- **Integration with Wearables** for real-time health data.
